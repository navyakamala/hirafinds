# Privacy Policy for Hira Finds

Last updated: 30 October 2026

## Overview

Hira Finds ("we", "our") operates a small personal automation tool that publishes pins to our own Pinterest business account. The tool is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public or to other users.

## What the tool does

- Creates pins that promote books we have self-published on Amazon KDP and handmade crochet products we sell.
- Publishes those pins to boards on our own Pinterest account.
- Reads our own boards and pins so it can choose the correct board and avoid duplicate posts.

## What data we collect and store

- The tool accesses only the data of the Pinterest account that owns it.
- We do not access, collect, store, sell or share personal data about any other Pinterest user.
- The only credential stored is our own Pinterest access token, which is kept privately and is never published or shared.
- The tool does not use cookies or tracking technologies and does not display ads.

## Third parties

The tool communicates with Pinterest through its official API, and Pinterest's own privacy policy applies to that data. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy policy.

## Affiliate links

In future, some pins may contain affiliate links. Where they do, we will clearly disclose this.

## Your rights and removal

Because we do not collect personal data about other people, there is no personal data to access or delete. If you have a question or concern, contact us at the email below.
